# CHAPTER 3: A FRAMEWORK FOR SYSTEM DESIGN INTERVIEWS
1. Steps
	1. Understand the problem and establish design scope
		1. 先透過家己的經驗來去了解問題的本質
		2. 理解問題時會使加互動，確定需求
		3. 將回應記錄，然後思考欲按怎處理
	2. Propose high-level design and get buy-in
		1. 先畫出簡單的設計圖，然後佮對方好好仔討論
		2. 共重要的部份畫出來就好
			1. 這需要經驗、慣勢、智識
				1. Technology Stack 無夠，無法度規畫
				2. 需要加強相關的 Domain Knowledge
			2. 模仿佮學習案例
		3. 深入討論進前，會記得先估算
			1. 估算時愛佮對方討論按呢算敢有適合，雙方有共識才沓沓仔深入
	3. Design deep dive
		1. 確定重要的技術環節，然後才閣繼續討論
			1. 避免講傷過深的部份
				1. 譬如需要開傷濟時間了解的電劃法，會使先跳過，因為對解說家己的設計概念可能無幫助。
	4. Wrap up
		1. 挑戰時間
			1. 對方會開始挑戰佮延伸設計
				1. 直面問題，永遠有改進空間
				2. 幫助對方回憶家己的設計
				3. 
		2. Advice Lists
			1. Dos
				• Always ask for clarification. Do not assume your assumption is correct.
				• Understand the requirements of the problem.
				• There is neither the right answer nor the best answer. A solution designed to solve the
				problems of a young startup is different from that of an established company with millions
				of users. Make sure you understand the requirements.
				• Let the interviewer know what you are thinking. Communicate with your interview.
				• Suggest multiple approaches if possible.
				• Once you agree with your interviewer on the blueprint, go into details on each
				component. Design the most critical components first.
				• Bounce ideas off the interviewer. A good interviewer works with you as a teammate.
				• Never give up.
			2. Don’ts
				• Don't be unprepared for typical interview questions.
				• Don’t jump into a solution without clarifying the requirements and assumptions.
				• Don’t go into too much detail on a single component in the beginning. Give the high-
				level design first then drills down.
				• If you get stuck, don't hesitate to ask for hints.
				• Again, communicate. Don't think in silence.
				• Don’t think your interview is done once you give the design. You are not done until your
				interviewer says you are done. Ask for feedback early and often.
2. 時間管理
	1. 絕對無可能佇短時間做出非常完整的設計，所以愛細膩分配時間，以下是用 45 分落去分配，主要猶是愛看對方予你的時間
	2. 大概時間
		1. Step 1 Understand the problem and establish design scope: 3 - 10 minutes
		2. Step 2 Propose high-level design and get buy-in: 10 - 15 minutes
		3. Step 3 Design deep dive: 10 - 25 minutes
		4. Step 4 Wrap: 3 - 5 minutes
	3. 個人整理的應對
		1. 先快速理解問題
		2. 佮對方配合、討論，做出雙方會使接受的設計
		3. 針對頭前的設計，確定對方想欲探討的細節部份
		4. 整理所有內容，然後等待對方問問題
