### What I’d advise

- **Cytoscape.js** if you want a solid, long-term core with lots of built-in layouts, good docs, and active releases. It’s historically SVG/canvas, and now has **WebGL rendering** in v3.31+ to boost large graphs, plus first-party TypeScript—nice for SPA apps. ([blog.js.cytoscape.org](https://blog.js.cytoscape.org/ "Cytoscape.js · News & tutorials"))
    
- **Sigma.js** if your priority is **speed with big graphs** and smooth interactions (WebGL by default). It pairs with **graphology** for data/algorithms and is great when you expect thousands of nodes/edges and need fluid zooming. ([sigmajs.org](https://www.sigmajs.org/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Sigma.js"))
    
- **vis-network (vis.js)** if you want the **quickest path** to a working, physics-based network view that’s easy to configure and OK up to a few thousand elements; good community docs, dual MIT/Apache license. ([visjs.github.io](https://visjs.github.io/vis-network/docs/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "vis.js - Network documentation. - GitHub Pages"))
    
- **AntV G6** if you want a **graph-editor feel** with rich presets and styling (popular in Asia). It’s flexible and has many graph scenarios covered out of the box. ([g6.antv.antgroup.com](https://g6.antv.antgroup.com/en?utm_source=chatgpt.com "G6 Graph Visualization Framework in JavaScript | AntV"))
    

### Who’s most used (in practice)

- **Cytoscape.js** has long, steady adoption across science and apps and is actively maintained (frequent 2025 releases + WebGL preview). If you want “safe and proven,” this is it. ([blog.js.cytoscape.org](https://blog.js.cytoscape.org/ "Cytoscape.js · News & tutorials"))
    
- **Sigma.js** is a go-to when teams specifically need **WebGL performance** for large networks. Stars + ecosystem (graphology) reflect that niche. ([GitHub](https://github.com/jacomyal/sigma.js "GitHub - jacomyal/sigma.js: A JavaScript library aimed at visualizing graphs of thousands of nodes and edges"))
    
- **vis-network** remains popular because it’s **simple to get results** and handles a few thousand elements with clustering; the docs are friendly. ([GitHub](https://github.com/visjs/vis-network "GitHub - visjs/vis-network: :dizzy: Display dynamic, automatically organised, customizable network views."))
    
- **G6** is widely used in enterprise dashboards (especially China-based products) when people want editor-like interactions and polished visuals. ([g6.antv.antgroup.com](https://g6.antv.antgroup.com/en?utm_source=chatgpt.com "G6 Graph Visualization Framework in JavaScript | AntV"))
    

### Quick selector (based on your likely needs)

- **Hundreds → low thousands of nodes, want rich layouts + steady ecosystem:** Cytoscape.js. ([blog.js.cytoscape.org](https://blog.js.cytoscape.org/ "Cytoscape.js · News & tutorials"))
    
- **Thousands+ nodes and you care most about rendering performance:** Sigma.js. ([sigmajs.org](https://www.sigmajs.org/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Sigma.js"))
    
- **You want to stand up something fast with physics and decent defaults:** vis-network. ([visjs.github.io](https://visjs.github.io/vis-network/docs/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "vis.js - Network documentation. - GitHub Pages"))
    
- **You want an editor/workbench UX (drag to connect, rich node cards) out of the box:** G6. ([g6.antv.antgroup.com](https://g6.antv.antgroup.com/en?utm_source=chatgpt.com "G6 Graph Visualization Framework in JavaScript | AntV"))
